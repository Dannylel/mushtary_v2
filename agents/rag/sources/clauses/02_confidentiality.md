# Confidentiality & Data Protection

Confidentiality of tender information. All information exchanged between the buyer and bidders in connection with this tender shall be treated as strictly confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party. This obligation survives the conclusion of the tender and any resulting contract.

Use limitation. Confidential information shall be used solely for the purpose of preparing a proposal, performing the contract, or as otherwise expressly permitted in writing. Neither party shall use the other's confidential information for any competing, commercial, or unrelated purpose.

Protection standard. Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using at least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than a reasonable standard of care.

Personal data. Where the engagement involves personal data, the vendor shall process such data only on documented instructions from the buyer, implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, and comply with applicable data-protection laws and regulations.

Permitted disclosure. A party may disclose confidential information to the extent required by law, regulation, or competent authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives prompt notice to the other party to allow it to seek protective measures.

Return or destruction. Upon completion or termination of the engagement, or upon request, the vendor shall return or securely destroy all confidential information and copies thereof, and certify such destruction in writing where required.

Public announcements. The vendor shall not issue any public announcement, press release, or marketing reference concerning the buyer or the engagement without the buyer's prior written approval.
