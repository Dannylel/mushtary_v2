# Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

Legal standing. Bidders shall be legally registered and licensed to conduct the relevant activity, hold a valid commercial registration, and maintain all licenses, memberships, and registrations required to perform the work. Registered business activities shall be consistent with the subject of the tender.

Financial standing. Bidders shall demonstrate sound financial standing sufficient to perform the contract, evidenced where required by audited financial statements, bank certificates, or equivalent documentation for the stated period.

Technical capability. Bidders shall demonstrate the technical capability, resources, and qualified personnel necessary to perform the work, including relevant certifications and accreditations applicable to the category.

Relevant experience. Bidders shall demonstrate a track record of similar projects, meeting any stated minimum number of comparable engagements, minimum years of experience, and minimum individual project value, supported by references or completion certificates.

Key personnel. Bidders shall propose suitably qualified key personnel and shall not substitute them without the buyer's prior written approval and with personnel of equivalent or superior qualifications.

Tax and statutory compliance. Bidders shall be current with their tax, social-insurance, and other statutory obligations, and shall provide valid certificates of compliance where required.

Good standing and integrity. Bidders shall not be subject to debarment, suspension, or blacklisting, and shall declare that they have not engaged in fraud, corruption, collusion, or coercive practices in connection with any tender.

Joint ventures and consortia. Where a joint venture or consortium submits a proposal, all members shall be jointly and severally liable, a lead member shall be designated, and the qualification requirements shall be met collectively as specified in the tender.
