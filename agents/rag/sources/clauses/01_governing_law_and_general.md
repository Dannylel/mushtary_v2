# General Terms & Governing Law

Governing law. This Request for Proposal (RFP) and any contract resulting from it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The parties shall comply with all relevant statutory, regulatory, and licensing requirements throughout the engagement.

Acceptance of conditions. Submission of a proposal constitutes the bidder's full acceptance of the terms, conditions, and instructions of this tender, unless specific exceptions are clearly and separately declared in writing within the proposal.

No binding relationship until signature. No binding contractual relationship shall exist between the buyer and any bidder until a formal written agreement has been signed and executed by the duly authorized representatives of both parties. The issuance of this RFP does not oblige the buyer to award a contract.

Buyer reservations. The buyer reserves the right, at its sole discretion and without incurring any liability, to amend, suspend, extend, or cancel this tender; to reject any or all proposals; to award in whole or in part; to negotiate with one or more bidders; and to waive minor informalities or irregularities in any proposal.

Costs of participation. Each bidder shall bear all costs associated with the preparation and submission of its proposal. The buyer shall under no circumstances be responsible or liable for those costs, regardless of the outcome of the tender.

Conflict of interest. Bidders shall disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest. The buyer may disqualify any bidder found to have a conflict of interest that cannot be adequately mitigated.

Indemnification. The vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the buyer, its officers, and its employees against any claims, losses, damages, or liabilities arising from the vendor's breach, negligence, or wilful misconduct in the performance of the contract.

Force majeure. Neither party shall be liable for failure or delay in performance to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided the affected party gives prompt written notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

Assignment and subcontracting. The vendor shall not assign, transfer, or subcontract any part of the contract without the prior written consent of the buyer. Approved subcontracting does not relieve the vendor of its obligations or liability under the contract.

Notices. All formal notices under the contract shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses and contacts designated by each party, and shall be deemed received upon documented delivery.
