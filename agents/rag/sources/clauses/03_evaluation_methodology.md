# Evaluation Methodology

Two-envelope evaluation. Proposals shall be submitted and evaluated in two separate parts: a technical proposal and a commercial (financial) proposal. The commercial proposal of a bidder shall be opened and considered only after the technical proposal has been evaluated.

Compliance gate. Prior to scoring, each proposal shall be screened against the mandatory and eligibility requirements. Proposals that fail any mandatory requirement shall be excluded from further evaluation and shall not be scored.

Technical evaluation. The technical proposal shall be assessed against the published technical parameters, which may include completeness of the submission; clarity, organization, and internal consistency; alignment of the proposed solution with the scope of work and objectives; soundness and realism of the proposed methodology and work plan; qualifications and experience of key personnel; relevant track record; and the realism and achievability of the proposed timeline and resourcing.

Minimum technical threshold. A proposal must achieve the stated minimum technical score to be considered technically qualified. Proposals scoring below the threshold shall not proceed to commercial evaluation.

Commercial evaluation. The commercial proposal shall be assessed for completeness and arithmetical correctness, total proposed price and itemized cost breakdown, value for money rather than lowest price alone, and cost realism, including the absence of abnormally low or unbalanced pricing.

Combined scoring. The overall result shall combine technical and commercial scores in accordance with the published weighting (for example, technical and financial weights summing to one hundred percent). The weighting and minimum thresholds are fixed by the buyer and are not subject to negotiation.

Award basis. The contract shall be awarded to the most economically advantageous compliant proposal, determined on the basis of the published evaluation model and weighting, subject to the bidder passing all mandatory criteria and meeting the minimum technical score.

Clarifications. The buyer may seek written clarification of any proposal during evaluation. Clarifications shall not permit a bidder to materially change its technical solution or price after the submission deadline.

Confidentiality of evaluation. The deliberations and records of the evaluation committee shall remain confidential. Each proposal shall be evaluated on its own merits and shall not be disclosed to, or compared in the presence of, competing bidders.
