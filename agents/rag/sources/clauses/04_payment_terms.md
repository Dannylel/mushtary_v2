# Payment Terms

Payment basis. Payments shall be made on a milestone or deliverable basis, subject to the buyer's written acceptance of each milestone or deliverable. No payment shall become due in respect of work that has not been accepted by the buyer.

Invoicing requirements. Each invoice shall be a valid tax invoice issued by the vendor and shall be accompanied by a signed milestone-acceptance or completion certificate and any supporting documentation required by the buyer. Vendor banking details shall match the approved bank-account certificate on file.

Payment timeline. Properly submitted, complete, and buyer-approved invoices shall be processed within thirty (30) calendar days of approval, unless a different period is stated in the contract.

Advance payment. Any advance payment, if permitted, shall be secured by an advance-payment guarantee of equivalent value, valid until the advance is fully recovered through deductions from subsequent payments.

Retention. The buyer may withhold a retention from each payment, up to the stated retention percentage, to be released upon final acceptance and the satisfactory completion of all contractual obligations, including any warranty or defect-liability period.

Taxes and duties. Quoted prices shall be inclusive of all applicable taxes, duties, and charges except value-added tax, which shall be shown separately where applicable. The vendor is responsible for its own tax compliance and remittances.

Currency and price firmness. Prices shall be quoted in Saudi Riyals (SAR) and shall remain firm and fixed for the validity period of the proposal and the duration of the contract, unless a price-adjustment mechanism is expressly agreed.

Set-off. The buyer may set off against any amount payable to the vendor any sums owed by the vendor to the buyer, including liquidated damages, penalties, and recoveries.
