# Delivery, Acceptance & Warranty

Delivery and performance. The vendor shall deliver the goods, services, or works in accordance with the agreed scope, specifications, and timeline, and in compliance with applicable standards and good industry practice.

Acceptance procedure. Deliverables shall be subject to the buyer's review and written acceptance against the agreed acceptance criteria. The buyer may accept, conditionally accept with a defect list, or reject a deliverable that does not conform, requiring the vendor to remedy it within a reasonable period at no additional cost.

Inspection and testing. The buyer may inspect, test, or audit the work and the vendor's performance. Inspection or testing does not relieve the vendor of its obligations or warranties.

Warranty. The vendor warrants that the goods, services, or works shall conform to the specifications, be free from material defects, and be fit for their intended purpose for the stated warranty or defect-liability period commencing from final acceptance.

Defect remedy during warranty. During the warranty period, the vendor shall, at its own cost and within the agreed response and resolution times, repair or replace defective items and correct defective work attributable to the vendor.

Intellectual property. Unless otherwise agreed, deliverables produced specifically for the buyer shall vest in the buyer upon payment. The vendor shall grant the buyer the licenses necessary to use any pre-existing or third-party materials embedded in the deliverables.

IP indemnity. The vendor warrants that the deliverables do not infringe the intellectual-property rights of any third party and shall indemnify the buyer against claims of infringement arising from the deliverables.

Transition and handover. Upon completion or termination, the vendor shall provide an orderly handover, including documentation, source materials, credentials, and knowledge transfer necessary for the buyer or a successor to continue operations.

Records and audit. The vendor shall maintain accurate records relating to the contract and shall, on reasonable notice, permit the buyer to audit those records for compliance and payment-verification purposes.
