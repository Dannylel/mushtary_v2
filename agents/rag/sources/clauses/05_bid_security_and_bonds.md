# Bid Security, Performance Bonds & Guarantees

Bid security (bid bond). Where required, each bidder shall submit a bid security in the form and amount stated in the tender, valid for the full proposal-validity period. Proposals not accompanied by a compliant bid security shall be rejected. The bid security shall be issued by an institution acceptable to the buyer.

Forfeiture of bid security. The bid security may be forfeited if a bidder withdraws its proposal during the validity period, refuses to accept a correction of arithmetical errors, fails to sign the contract when called upon, or fails to provide the required performance bond within the stipulated period.

Release of bid security. The bid security of unsuccessful bidders shall be released after award or expiry of the validity period. The successful bidder's bid security shall be released upon submission of an acceptable performance bond and signature of the contract.

Performance bond. The successful bidder shall, within the period stated in the tender, submit a performance bond equal to the stated percentage of the contract value, valid throughout the implementation period and any warranty or defect-liability period, and renewable on demand until obligations are fully discharged.

Drawing on the performance bond. The buyer may draw upon the performance bond, in whole or in part, to recover losses arising from the vendor's default, delay, or failure to perform, without prejudice to any other remedy available to the buyer.

Advance-payment guarantee. Where an advance payment is made, the vendor shall provide an advance-payment guarantee of equal value, reduced proportionally as the advance is recovered, and valid until full recovery.

Warranty / retention guarantee. In lieu of cash retention, the buyer may, at its discretion, accept a warranty guarantee of equivalent value covering the warranty or defect-liability period.

Validity and renewal. All guarantees shall remain valid and enforceable for their required periods. The vendor shall renew any guarantee prior to its expiry where obligations remain outstanding; failure to do so entitles the buyer to draw the guarantee in full.
