# Submission, Validity & Award

Submission method. Proposals shall be submitted only through the designated platform or method stated in the tender, in the required format, before the submission deadline. Proposals received after the deadline shall not be accepted.

Proposal format. Unless otherwise stated, bidders shall submit separate technical and commercial proposals in the required file formats. Pricing shall not appear anywhere in the technical proposal.

Proposal validity. Proposals shall remain valid and open for acceptance for the stated validity period from the submission deadline. The buyer may request an extension of validity, which a bidder may accept without changing its price.

Clarifications during the tender period. Bidders may request clarifications up to the stated clarification deadline. The buyer's responses, and any addenda, shall be issued to all bidders and shall form part of the tender documents.

Amendments and addenda. The buyer may amend the tender documents by written addendum at any time before the deadline. Where necessary, the buyer may extend the deadline to allow bidders to account for an addendum.

Late, incomplete, or non-compliant submissions. Late submissions shall be rejected. Incomplete or non-compliant submissions may be rejected or, at the buyer's discretion, subject to clarification for minor deficiencies that do not affect substance, price, or fairness.

Opening. Proposals shall be opened in accordance with the stated procedure. The timestamp recorded by the submission platform shall be the official record of submission time.

Notification and award. The buyer shall notify the successful bidder of the award and may notify unsuccessful bidders of the outcome. Award is conditional upon the successful bidder providing the required guarantees and signing the contract within the stipulated period.

Standstill and review. Where applicable, the buyer shall observe any required standstill period before signing, during which aggrieved bidders may seek review in accordance with the applicable regulations.

Failure to sign. If the successful bidder fails to sign the contract or provide the required guarantees within the stipulated period, the buyer may forfeit the bid security and award to the next-ranked compliant bidder or re-tender.
