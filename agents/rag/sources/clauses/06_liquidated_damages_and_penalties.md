# Delay, Liquidated Damages & Penalties

Liquidated damages for delay. If the vendor fails to complete the work or deliver a milestone by the agreed date for reasons attributable to the vendor, the buyer may apply liquidated damages at the rate stated in the contract for each period of delay, up to the stated maximum percentage of the contract value. Liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of loss and not a penalty.

Cap on liquidated damages. The aggregate liquidated damages for delay shall not exceed the maximum percentage of the contract value stated in the contract. Reaching the cap entitles the buyer to terminate the contract for default.

Service-level penalties. Where service levels apply, failure to meet a committed service level may result in service credits or penalties calculated as stated in the service-level schedule, without prejudice to the buyer's other rights.

Deduction. The buyer may deduct liquidated damages, service credits, and penalties from any amounts due to the vendor or recover them from the performance bond or other guarantees.

Continued performance. The application of liquidated damages or penalties does not relieve the vendor of its obligation to complete the work, nor does it waive any other right or remedy of the buyer.

Remedy and cure. Before exercising certain remedies, the buyer may issue a written notice requiring the vendor to remedy a default within a reasonable cure period; failure to cure entitles the buyer to proceed with the remedies available under the contract.

Termination for default. The buyer may terminate the contract, in whole or in part, if the vendor commits a material breach that is not cured within the notice period, becomes insolvent, or engages in fraud, corruption, or collusion. Upon termination for default, the buyer may procure substitute performance and recover additional costs from the vendor.

Termination for convenience. The buyer may terminate the contract for convenience upon written notice, in which case the vendor shall be paid for work satisfactorily performed and accepted up to the date of termination, with no further liability on the buyer.
