# Compliance, Localization & Conduct

Regulatory compliance. The vendor shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, technical standards, and licensing requirements relevant to the work, and shall maintain such compliance throughout the contract.

Localization and local content. The vendor shall comply with applicable local-content and localization requirements, including any minimum local-content thresholds, preference mechanisms, and reporting obligations stipulated by the buyer or the applicable regulations.

Workforce localization (Saudization). Where required, the vendor shall meet the applicable national-workforce (Saudization) requirements for the duration of the contract and provide evidence of compliance on request.

Health, safety, and environment. The vendor shall perform the work in a manner that protects health, safety, and the environment, and shall comply with the buyer's site rules and applicable HSE regulations.

Anti-corruption and integrity. The vendor shall not offer, give, or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper advantage, and shall not engage in fraudulent, collusive, or coercive practices. Breach of this clause entitles the buyer to terminate the contract and pursue all available remedies.

Ethical conduct and non-discrimination. The vendor shall conduct itself ethically, treat personnel fairly, and comply with applicable labor laws, including those concerning wages, working conditions, and non-discrimination.

Insurance. The vendor shall maintain, at its own cost, the insurance coverage required by the contract, including any required liability, workers' compensation, and professional-indemnity coverage, and shall provide evidence of coverage on request.

Sanctions and export control. The vendor warrants that it is not subject to applicable sanctions and shall comply with applicable export-control and sanctions regulations in performing the contract.

Independent contractor. The vendor is an independent contractor. Nothing in the contract creates a partnership, agency, or employment relationship between the parties.

Survival. Provisions that by their nature should survive termination — including confidentiality, intellectual property, indemnities, warranties, and audit rights — shall remain in effect after the contract ends.
